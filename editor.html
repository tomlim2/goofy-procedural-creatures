    Species first, then parts · the rules are reported, not applied — what you pick is drawn · PART: pick a part by its picture; SHAPE is one line — click it and the forms drop down, each its picture then its name — its properties (its own sizes, lengths and manners) stand open under it, and MATERIAL, one line the same way, comes last · + at the top of a MATERIAL list opens NEW MATERIAL — its texture, density and colour, drawn on the creature as you pick, then MAKE or CANCEL — and ✎ at the right of a material's row opens the same on that material, SAVE or CANCEL · MATERIALS shows every surface the creature wears — click a ball to edit that one and see which parts take it; each surface is a material of its own · HAND is the hand that draws — a new one, and its wobble · SAVE writes the whole spec — the file the board's pin opens into a cell · R shuffle · B pose
